title: Enchiladas

|| Ingredients |
|-:|-|
1 C   | salsa and green chilis combo
½ C   | sour cream
2 tsp | chili powder
2 C   | cooked chicken, cut in small cubes
½ C   | shredded monterey jack cheese (about 2 ounces)
6     | flour tortillas (8-inch), warmed
1     | green onion, thinly sliced (about 2 tablespoons)

## Directions

1. Stir salsa mix, sour cream and chili powder in a large bowl.
2. Add chicken and cheese.
3. Spoon about ⅓ cup chicken mixture in a line, down the center of each tortilla.
   1. Roll up the tortillas around the mixture.
   2. Place seam-side up in a lightly greased 11 x 8-inch shallow baking dish.
   3. Top with sprinkle of extra cheese.
   4. Cover with foil.
4. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes or until the enchiladas are hot and bubbling.
5. Top with the green onion.
