|| Ingredients |
|-:|-|
3 C     | whole milk
½ C     | butter, unsalted
½ C     | flour
2 tsp   | kosher salt
1 lb    | pasta
2 - 3 C | shredded cheese (see appendix)
&nbsp;  | meat or other mix-ins (see appendix)

## Directions

1. Start boiling water, add pasta when boiling. Then drain and rinse.
2. Make the sauce base:
  1. In a small pot, heat milk until barely bubbling, then remove.
  2. In a large heavy pot (ie dutch oven), melt butter.
  3. Add flour and whisk constantly for 3 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat to add the milk, slowly, 1 cup at a time, while whisking.
  5. Return to heat, and whisk for another 3 minutes.
  6. Add the salt.
4. Add cheese to sauce base, cook 3 minutes, until melted.
5. Add any mix-ins.
6. Add the pasta, cook 5 minutes.

> Adapted from the Homeroom restaurant cookbook

### Appendix

* The sauce base can be made in advance, stored in fridge, and re-warmed later.
* Baking option: Skip last 5 minute cook step. Instead, transfer to baking dish, top with panko, bake 10 mins at 400 degrees.
* Cheese / Mix-in suggestions:
  * 3:1 Cheddar to Pecorino romano.
  * 4:1 Cheddar to Cream cheese. With jalapeno. (Baked)
  * 1:1 Smoked cheddar to Jack cheese. With bacon.
  * 3:1 Gouda to Pecorino romano. With garlic butter.
  * 1:1 Jack to Pecorino romano. With pepper.
  * Jack cheese. With chorizo, chipotle chillis in adobo. Cilantro topping.
