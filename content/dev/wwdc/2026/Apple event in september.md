## iPhone 18 Pro
* Color matched aluminum and back-glass
* New colors: glacier and burgundy
* All Apple intelligence features discussed for iOS 27
* A20 Pro chip
* New thermal design with chip/memory and other components rearranged
* Main camera has a physical aperture
* Apple Reference photo:
  * A signed version of the original photo taken, which could be used to prove an image was not AI generated
* Smaller dynamic island
* iPhone handoff: Use a single phone number on two different iPhones, switching active when one is picked up.
* Available Sept 18

## Airpods 5
* Open-ear (no tips) fit
* Active noise cancellation is much better
* Live translation
* No price increase
* Enhanced version also: with a volume slider, wireless charger, and bigger battery

## Apple watch series 12 (and ultra 4)
* Health sensing system
  * Bigger electrodes
  * Bigger light samplers
  * Much more frequent sampling for heart rate and HRV
* New metric: Readiness, based on activity, sleep, etc
* Intelligence surfacing important changes in the Health app, changed design.
* Health Age: Metrics compared to average age targets
  * Can integrate results from bloodwork labs
* Tests (eg VO2max) uses camera to track that you do the motions correctly, while analyzing your metrics
* Audio intelligence
  * Like visual intelilgnce but for sounds
  * Sound recognition
    * eg get a notification for a siren or doorbell
  * Especially important for deaf or hard of hearing
  * 15 second live rewind
  * Siri recap, overview of meetings or discussions. Uses AI to take notes and summarize. Can be schedule or always or only manually.
* Available in a ceramic shell in addition to standard metals.
* Available Sept 18

## iPhone Duo
* The expected foldable iphone
* "Passport" sized
  * So, wider than a normal phone. Seems awkwardly wide in the photos.
  * Unfolds into an ipad mini-like size
  * Same 1 x 1.4 aspect ratio for both displays
  * 7.6" inside display. 5.4" outer display.
* Very thin, like last year's iphone air. All parts are in the camera bump
* Controls move to the side, instead of top and bottom.
  * Dock, status bars, etc.
* Can do partial folds: eg for keyboard mode, or use itself as a stand
* Will support apple pencil
* Touch ID returns, to the side button
* Apps have been optimized for the new screen size.
  * This changes some of the accepted design paradigms for iOS.
  * Can have dual home screen, swapping apps on each half of the large screen.
* Display
  * Fancy nanotexture finish hides crease
  * Multiple layers that slide like pages of a book
  * Fancy hinge, with custom printed layers per device during manufacturing
  * Front-facing camera is under display? Maybe that part goes dark when camera is being used.
* Two batteries, split across the halves
* Accessories, Kickstand folio, and wraparound case
* Starts at $1999, available Oct 23
